# KKOKKAPICK launch marketing plan — KRW 100,000/month

Updated: 2026-09-29
Owner role: Online Marketing Lead
Status: planning only; no spend/account activation authorized

## Product understanding
KKOKKAPICK is not positioned as another kidswear mall. Its acquisition story is: discover baby/kids clothing across sources, then reduce size-choice uncertainty with KKOKKAFIT where verified evidence exists. Marketing must show the evidence boundary: a recommendation tells the parent which size to check first, not that a SKU is definitely in stock or guaranteed to fit.

## Primary early audience
Start narrow: Korean mobile-shopping parents/caregivers of babies and toddlers who repeatedly search multiple malls and hesitate over brand-specific sizing. Secondary audience is gift buyers, but do not spend the first paid budget on both audiences.

## Message hypotheses
H1 — pain: “아기옷, 브랜드마다 사이즈가 달라서 어렵다면.”
H2 — utility: “아이 키·몸무게로 먼저 확인할 사이즈를 빠르게.”
H3 — discovery: “여러 아기옷을 한곳에서 보고, 사이즈 근거까지.”
Do not advertise “정확한 사이즈 보장”, “최저가 보장”, or unsupported catalog breadth.

## Organic-first plan
- Create short demonstrations around one concrete job: same child, different brand size guides; show how KKOKKAFIT explains its recommendation.
- Build searchable evergreen pages/posts for brand + baby size-guide intent only when first-party evidence is verified.
- Reuse each demonstration as owned short-form/social/community material; avoid spam or disguised promotion.
- Add product-native share surfaces later: share a product/fit evidence page, never expose child measurements in the share payload.
- Prepare store listing screenshots around discovery -> child profile -> evidence -> merchant handoff.

## KRW 100,000 paid experiment
Do not fragment across multiple networks.

Phase A, KRW 30,000: one channel, 2-3 creatives/messages, same audience/landing experience. Goal is message validation, not scale.
Phase B, KRW 50,000: put budget only behind the best qualified-activation creative from Phase A.
Reserve KRW 20,000: one retest of a materially different creative/landing hypothesis or hold unspent if tracking/sample quality is poor.

Channel selection is deferred until launch surfaces and measurement are ready. Prefer the channel where install/open and downstream activation can be measured with minimal privacy/data overhead. Do not activate spend before Product Owner approval.

## Funnel
1. Ad/organic impression
2. Store/landing visit
3. Install or first open
4. Product discovery engagement
5. Child profile completed
6. Verified KKOKKAFIT product viewed
7. Fit recommendation viewed
8. Merchant handoff
9. Return visit / favorite / price-alert activation

North-star launch learning metric: cost per qualified activation, where qualified activation requires meaningful product discovery plus either a verified KKOKKAFIT interaction or a high-intent save/merchant handoff. Exact event definition must be frozen before paid launch.

## Stop / continue rules
- Stop a creative if it attracts clicks but produces no qualified activations after enough tracked traffic to diagnose the funnel; do not optimize CTR alone.
- Stop paid acquisition entirely if attribution is unreliable, the landing/store experience is materially broken, or verified KKOKKAFIT inventory is too thin to support the advertised promise.
- Continue only messages that improve downstream qualified activation without misleading claims.
- Never increase the monthly cap automatically.

## Pre-launch deliverables
- Freeze launch ICP and 3 message variants.
- Produce 3 vertical short-form creative storyboards and store screenshot copy.
- Define privacy-safe event taxonomy and attribution boundary.
- Establish baseline organic traffic/activation before interpreting paid lift.
- Build a weekly one-page experiment report: spend, qualified activations, funnel drop-off, learnings, next action.

## Dependencies / gates
Paid launch depends on stable production URL/store listing, production analytics/crash boundary, sufficient verified catalog/fit coverage, legal/support identity, provider/trademark rights, and explicit Product Owner spend authorization.


## Future AI try-on growth hypothesis
AI virtual try-on is a candidate post-core-release acquisition/conversion hook, not part of the initial KRW 100k paid plan. Do not advertise it until the privacy/security/provider gates in docs/ai-virtual-tryon-feasibility.md pass and a production-quality beta exists. If approved later, test qualified merchant handoff and paid conversion lift against a control; generation count or shares alone are not success metrics.
