# Mobile UX direction

Target: parents in their 30s–40s who need to scan many baby/kids apparel options quickly with one hand.

Reference principles are adapted, not copied: low learning cost and strong hierarchy from familiar local marketplace patterns; child-stage personalization and reassuring parenting context from parenting apps.

## Product rules
- The child context is visible before merchandising when a profile exists.
- Search is the primary action; category and stage filters remain immediately reachable.
- Product cards prioritize image → brand/name → payable price → merchant comparison → fit evidence.
- KKOKKAFIT uses evidence language, never certainty language without a verified manufacturer chart.
- Sponsored content is visually distinct and never mixed into organic ranking.
- Favorites and price alerts are persistent-value actions; sign-in is requested only when cross-device persistence or notification delivery is needed.
- Minimum primary touch target is 48dp. Dense secondary metadata must not compete with price or fit status.
- Warm surfaces are allowed, but decorative color must not encode fit or commercial trust.

## Navigation target
External beta should converge on four stable destinations: 홈, 검색, 찜, 마이. Product detail remains a focused sheet/page. Child profile and account/privacy controls live under 마이, while the home surface exposes a compact active-child context.
